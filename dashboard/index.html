Install Open Brain

Put Open Brain on your phone's Home Screen so it opens like an app.
1
Open the browser menu or Share menu
On iPhone/iPad, tap the Share button. On Android, open the browser menu.
2
Choose Add to Home Screen or Install app
The wording varies slightly by browser.
3
Confirm Open Brain
It will appear with the Open Brain icon and launch in its own app-style window.
