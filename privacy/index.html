Open Brain

Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This notice explains how Open Brain handles information when you use the family dashboard and its optional Google Calendar and Gmail connections. Open Brain is operated for personal and household use. For support or privacy questions, contact kayla.packer@gmail.com.

Information used by Open Brain

Open Brain processes your account and household membership details and information you enter, such as tasks, reminders, habits, lists, memories, events, weekly plans, private Health & Wellbeing entries, and notification preferences such as quiet hours and time zone, and your personal assistant settings such as its name, tone, response length, planning notes and optional working hours. The app uses this information to provide the features you choose.

Household information is available according to the app's sharing and membership controls. Health & Wellbeing entries are private to their owner and are not included in the general AI assistant context.

Google Calendar information

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. With your permission, Open Brain reads your calendar list, calendar names and display settings, and events from calendars you select. Event information may include titles, start and end times, all-day status, locations, descriptions, and Google event links. The primary calendar address is used as the connection label.

For display and Calendar questions, Open Brain requests these read-only permissions:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly: list calendars so you can choose what to display.
  • calendar.events.readonly: read events for your dashboard.

These read-only permissions do not allow Google event changes. You may separately enable reviewed event creation with calendar.events, a permission that technically allows creating, changing and deleting events on calendars you can write to. Open Brain uses it here only to create the specific event you review and approve. It does not expose editing or deletion of existing Google events. Google events are displayed for the connected user and are not automatically copied into shared household events.

Google data use and sharing

The optional Gmail pilot requests only gmail.readonly. When you explicitly run a search or open a message source, Open Brain reads bounded message headers and short excerpts for your own account. It does not read attachments, change mail, create Gmail drafts or send email. Email excerpts are held in the current browser view, are cleared on reload/sign-out, and are not sent to the general AI assistant or saved as memories. Keyword flags indicate possible deadlines or commitments. An on-demand review extracts supported date text with its source evidence; these suggestions remain unconfirmed and no due time is inferred. You choose any due date, time and work estimate.

Preparing a private reminder creates a proposal for your review. If you approve it, the task and private activity draft retain its title, email subject, sender, Gmail link, chosen due time/estimate and any event reference you deliberately select (title, start time, event ID and Google calendar ID when applicable); the excerpt is not copied. Saved private tasks may be used in the assistant's ordinary task context. These separate records remain until you remove them in their screens. Gmail tokens are encrypted server-side; other household members cannot access your connection. Disconnect revokes the Google grant and removes the local credential, but does not delete saved task/proposal copies. Google revocation may also require reconnecting Calendar for the same app/project/account.

Google Calendar information is used to display your schedule, answer calendar checks you explicitly request, and maintain the connection you requested. In email review, choosing Check selected calendars reads your selected calendars and visible household events to show possible related events and gaps. These checks use direct calculations, do not send the review to an AI model, and do not reserve time. Selected details are rechecked when a proposal is prepared; later changes can make that snapshot outdated. Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Fetched events are excluded from general assistant requests; the event reference you explicitly save in a private task may enter ordinary task context as disclosed above.

Open Brain uses Google to authorize and read Calendar information and Supabase to run the connection service and store its encrypted refresh token and settings. Calendar information is sent to your signed-in browser for display. Service providers process information as needed to operate these features.

Open Brain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Reviewed Google event creation stores your private event draft, destination/account identifiers, selected source identifiers, conflict-check snapshot and provider result in Supabase. No Google event is written while preparing a review. Approval rechecks the selected calendars and accessible household events, then sends the exact approved title, start/end, location and details to Google. An email reference is copied only if you select its inclusion; an email-derived title is visible in the review. Calendar owners and editors may see these details even with private event visibility. No guests or invitations are added, and the calendar’s default reminders apply. The review is calculated directly without sending email or calendar records to an AI model. Fixed event identifiers and stored results support retry recovery after interruptions, but calendars may change after a conflict check. Disconnect does not delete created Google events or these separate review/result records, which are retained until account deletion or administrator removal.

Storage and security

Account and app records are stored in Supabase. Google refresh tokens are encrypted before storage in a server-side table that normal browser clients cannot read. The browser does not receive the refresh token. Google event responses are marked not to be cached and are held in dashboard memory for display rather than saved as household event records. Explicit event-creation reviews retain the approved draft, the bounded conflict snapshot and save result as described above.

Your browser stores sign-in information, preferences, and cached app files to support the installed app experience. If you enable notifications, reminder content can appear on your device. Use device notification settings to control lock-screen visibility.

AI features

If you use the in-app assistant, your messages and relevant accessible Open Brain context are processed by the app's configured AI service providers to generate responses. Fetched Google Calendar events and private Health & Wellbeing entries are excluded from the general assistant context; explicitly saved task references follow the task-context behavior above. If you enable “Check my Google Calendar for this question,” the AI service interprets only your typed question and the household date/time to identify the requested window. Open Brain then reads your selected calendars and accessible family events and calculates the answer directly. Event details and the resulting calendar answer are not sent to the AI service, saved in chat history, or included in later assistant requests. The Calendar control resets after each question. Information you type directly into a message becomes part of that message.

Your assistant settings are private to your account. These preferences and the relevant accessible app records may be included in general assistant answers and briefs. You can pause saved-memory use to exclude saved memories from new general answers and briefs; preferences and other app sources still apply. Health information remains excluded.

Activity & approvals keeps a private history of request types, states and times, proposed record drafts, source record identifiers and save decisions/results. It does not save your original questions, AI answers, full fetched Google Calendar responses or Calendar answers in that history. Email proposals retain the source details and any event reference you explicitly select as described above. Approvals apply to one displayed proposal, and creating a memory prepares a search embedding through the existing AI provider. Pause/cancel holds pending changes but cannot recall a request already sent to a provider or undo a completed save. Changing assistant settings or visible saved memories makes older proposals unavailable for approval; it does not delete activity history. Removing a saved memory does not erase copies independently retained in proposal history or other records. Activity records are removed when the account is deleted.

Background routines

Background routines are optional and remain off until you configure and start one. Daily deadline reviews and deadline-change watches check only visible Open Brain tasks assigned to you, including task identifiers, titles and due times. They do not read email, Google Calendar, private Health records or task descriptions, make AI calls, or change tasks/events. The check uses deadlines from the past 30 days through your chosen upcoming window, with a maximum of 200 tasks. Stored private results and run/delivery history are retained for 30 days; settings and the current deadline comparison baseline remain until you delete the routine or account. Usage counts are retained for 35 days and are not reset by deleting a routine.

If you select phone/browser or Telegram alerts, Open Brain sends generic text that a private check is ready, without task titles. Existing device/task-notification settings, Telegram connection and personal quiet hours still apply. Provider acceptance does not prove device display. Alert channels whose send result is uncertain are not blindly resent. You can pause or delete a routine and its history; pending work/alerts stop, while already dispatched requests cannot be recalled. Fixed limits allow at most 30 check attempts and four alert batches per UTC day per account, with up to ten phone/browser devices per batch. These limits do not cap the hosting bill. The server can continue opted-in work while the app is closed. Run results are not added to general assistant context.

Your controls and retention

In Your assistant, you can inspect accessible saved memories and edit or remove memories you created. Other people's shared memories are read only. Archived memories are excluded from assistant answers. Changing settings or saved memories resets the assistant conversation and brief caches when each device next refreshes or checks settings. Removal does not erase information independently saved in tasks or other sources, information you type again, or previous processing by service providers.

You can change calendar selections or disconnect in Open Brain Settings. Disconnect attempts to revoke Google access and removes the saved connection after revocation succeeds or the token is already invalid. If revocation fails temporarily, the app shows an error and keeps the connection so you can retry.

You can also revoke Open Brain's access through your Google Account connections. Revoking at Google prevents further access; use Disconnect in Open Brain to remove its saved connection record.

Connection credentials and settings are retained while the connection remains active. Other app records remain until removed through available app controls or by the administrator. For help deleting your account or stored information, contact kayla.packer@gmail.com.
