Open Brain

A calmer place for family life

Open Brain is a private family and personal productivity app. Bring household tasks, reminders, habits, shopping lists, memories, family events, and weekly planning together in one dashboard.

Open Brain is intended for personal use by a household and people known to its administrator. Access to household information requires an authorized Open Brain account.

Open your dashboard

Your schedule, in one place

Optionally connect Google Calendar to see events from calendars you choose alongside your Open Brain events in Today, This Week, and Upcoming Events.

Google Calendar access is read-only. Open Brain requests permission to list your calendars and read their events. It does not create, change, or delete Google events. You can refresh events, change which calendars are displayed, or disconnect in Settings.

Personal information stays personal

Health & Wellbeing tracking is private to the signed-in user and is excluded from the general AI assistant context. Google Calendar data is used for the calendar display and is not added to the general AI assistant context.
